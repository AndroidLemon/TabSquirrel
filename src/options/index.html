Tab Squirrel Settings

When idle tabs close, and who writes their summaries.

Let Tab Squirrel read pages

Tab Squirrel reads the text of an idle tab to write its note. Reading happens on this device. Page text leaves it only if you pick Gemini or a remote server under Summaries. Without this, idle tabs stay open and get no note.
